# Measuring pictures

Scope: the pictures on the designer's measuring cards for every shower drawn on its tray (three sided, walk-in, walk-in with return, the corner layouts, panel and door). Mode: Operate. The single door and the bath screen keep their own pictures for now.

Audience and job: a homeowner on a phone, in the bathroom, with a laser and a tape, taking one reading per card. The picture must show where the laser stands, where the tape starts, where the number is read, and the number itself, at a glance.

Constraints: the engine's cards, order and words are fixed; positions are never invented; one action per picture; works from a short phone to the desktop frame, light and dark.

Direction: brief-pinned by Matt (12 November 2026), an architectural isometric of the room. Memorable moment: the reading point, a pulsing red ring where the laser lights the yellow blade, with the number in a navy pill beside a draughtsman's arrowed dimension.

Unresolved: whether the single-door and bath-screen decks move to the same room; whether the bottom rakes are read from the corner before the laser moves (a sequence change, Matt to decide).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The measuring card is a technical illustration, not a diagram: one pale architectural room, seen from one isometric angle, in which only the act of measuring has colour. It refuses the flat, face-on CAD drawing with grey slabs and dashed lines that these cards replaced.

OWN-WORLD: Pale stone room, white tray, clear green-edged glass, soft contact shadows, one light over the eye's left shoulder. Three meaning colours only: laser red that glows (light, never an edge), tape yellow with black ticks, measuring navy for arrows, points and the number pill. Thin grey physical edges; strokes fixed in screen pixels.

STORY: The customer sees their own shower shape, sees where the laser stands and where the tape runs, sees the solid start point and the red read ring, reads the number, types it, moves on, and never loses their bearings because the room and the angle never change.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A phone card: title and instruction above; the picture fills the space between them and the number box, framed close on the card's action at the deck's one angle; the dimension with arrows and the navy number pill is the largest mark in it; tiny readings (the 50mm wall gap, the 10mm glass line) shown in a lens with the number under it.

FORM: Isometric technical illustration, drawn in SVG from the design's shape, the only entry on the list because the client's brief pinned it. No concept roll was run, so there is no seed: none (direction pinned by the client brief, no roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
