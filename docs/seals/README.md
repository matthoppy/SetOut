# Seal gaps

CRL's own sheet of suggested gaps (clearances) for its shower door seals,
wipes and sweeps: `crl-avdb3078-suggested-gaps.pdf` (AVDB3078, 10/14), saved
on 14 November 2026 from crlaurence.com.au, the link Matt sent.

What SetOut takes from it, and where Matt's own numbers stand instead, is in
`data/crl/DEDUCTIONS.md`.

| Seal | CRL number (10mm glass) | CRL's suggested gap | SetOut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bubble seal, U with bulb | PCR10 | 6mm | 9 on the handle side, swinging both ways; 8 on the hinge side at the wall (Matt) |
| h seal, 'Y' inline panel seal with a soft leg, on the fixed panel | PCC10 (PCC8, PCC12) | 4.7mm | 5 (Matt) |
| Clear angle jamb, hinge or strike side, wall or panel | P12LJ | 4.7mm | 5 (Matt), siliconed to the wall |
| 90° strike side seal (CRL UK), on the fixed panel at a corner | PCJ10 (PCJ8) | not on this sheet | the 90° seal at a corner, 5 (Matt, 16 November 2026) |
| U with 90° vinyl fin | PC1090 (PC0890, PC1200) | 20mm (22 on 12mm) | not used: CRL makes it for sliding doors |
| U with leg and vinyl insert, on the fixed panel at 90° to the door | P090SJ (10mm only) | 4.7mm | not used: CRL US's match for PCJ10 |
| Bottom wipe with drip rail | P990WS (P956WS, P912WS) | 11mm, 17 over the SDT980 threshold (8mm glass: 10 and 16) | 17, and 16 on 8mm glass (Matt) |
