# SetOut brand identity

`setout-brand.html` is the brand book, version 1, November 2026. Open it in a
browser; its typefaces load from Google Fonts. It is also published, privately,
at https://claude.ai/artifact/73j7xC47RxADBsosBGVUCM.

It answers Matt's brief of 12 November 2026: three directions, a recommendation
and the system that follows from it.

- **Point** (recommended): the lowercase wordmark `setout.` ending in a square
  SetOut Mint point. The app icon is `s.` on Midnight Navy.
- **Pane**: an S drawn as five glass panes in plan, with frameless gaps.
- **Line**: SETOUT set as a dimension between drawing ticks.

The recommendation is Point, with Line's drawing language (dimension lines with
45° ticks, JetBrains Mono figures, title blocks) for drawings, sheets and
quotes.

The colours are the brief's four: Midnight Navy `#101D2B`, SetOut Mint
`#65D5C0`, Soft White `#F3F5F4` and Slate Grey `#65717D`. Mint is never text on
a light ground (1.62 to 1 on Soft White), so links there use Mint Deep
`#1C776C`, and the main button is Navy on Mint (9.60 to 1). The page lists every
supporting colour and pairing.

Status: a proposal. Matt has not yet chosen a direction, and none of it is
applied to the site, the workspace or the designer.
